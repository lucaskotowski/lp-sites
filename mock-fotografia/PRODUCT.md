# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (buildless, host anywhere) — matches the sibling `lp_mecanica_v1` project.

## Users

Primary user (the site owner): a working photographer who needs an online presence that both shows their work and turns visitors into paying inquiries.

Primary visitor: someone who needs a photographer for a specific occasion (assumed: weddings, portraits, events, or brand/product shoots) and is evaluating whether this photographer's style, and the decision to reach out, is right for them.

*Assumption:* no specific real client. This is a demonstrative/spec landing page — a conversion-focused portfolio template for photographers. Named facts below marked *assumption* are not confirmed by a client.

## Product Purpose

A single landing page that presents a selection of a photographer's work and drives the visitor to make contact / request a booking. The portfolio exists to build enough confidence to convert; **conversion is the primary goal**, portfolio display is in service of it — not the other way around.

Success = the visitor initiates contact (assumed channel: WhatsApp click-to-chat and/or an inquiry form).

## Positioning

*Assumption (spec piece — no real differentiator supplied):* the page should let a real photographer's style and body of work carry the differentiation. Copy and structure must be written so a real photographer can drop in their own niche, voice, and proof without the layout fighting them.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from social media (Instagram in particular for photographers), local search, or referral links, and decide within the page whether to enquire. The decision is style-led: they judge the photography first, then logistics (availability, location, price range, process).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no build step or framework.
- *Assumption:* page content in Brazilian Portuguese (consistent with the user's language and the sibling project). Confirm before treating as fixed.
- No real assets exist yet. Every image, name, testimonial, price, and contact detail is a placeholder to be replaced. Do not fabricate business facts presented as real.
- Placeholder photography should be free-license stock (e.g. Pexels / Unsplash), visibly a stand-in, chosen deliberately rather than left as grey boxes.

## Brand Commitments

None supplied. No name, logo, palette, or voice is fixed. Any identity created is a starting proposal a real photographer would replace.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real photos, logo, testimonials, client list, pricing, or contact details.
- Absences future work must not fabricate as real: client names, testimonials/reviews, published work credits, pricing, availability claims, awards or press.

## Conversion Behavior

*Assumption:* primary CTA is a WhatsApp click-to-chat link with a pre-filled message; a secondary inquiry form (name, occasion, date, message) may support it. Placeholder number until a real one is supplied.

## Product Principles

1. The photography is the argument — lead with images, let them be large and uncompressed-looking, and never bury them under chrome.
2. Every section moves the visitor toward contact; the CTA is never more than one scroll away.
3. Structure is a fill-in-the-blanks template: a real photographer can swap niche, images, copy, and proof without redesigning.
4. Never present placeholder facts (names, testimonials, prices, contact) as if they were real.
5. Stays a static, buildless site that is simple to host anywhere.

## Workflow Notes

- Per the user: during this project, do not produce text write-ups or screenshot logs of changes. Keep working communication concise.
